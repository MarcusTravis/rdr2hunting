    How prices work

    The fence pays a fixed share of each horse's base value, and the share grows as your bond with the horse does. Each card shows the payout at bond 1 and at bond 4, the max, which is what the Price sort uses.

    Notes

    • The horse fence is in Clemens Cove, run by the Davies brothers. It opens once you finish “Horse Flesh for Dinner” in chapter 3.
    • The fence at Emerald Ranch only takes wagons, not horses.
    • Payout is a share of the horse's base value: 4% at bond 0, 8% at bond 1, 12% at bond 2, 20% at bond 3 and 30% at bond 4.
    • A horse you bought can't be fenced at bond 0. Bond it at least once first.
    • Honor discounts at the stable change what you pay, not the base value the fence uses.
    • All prices are story mode.
    • Where a gift or bonus horse's payout isn't confirmed, the price is left blank.
    • Base values come from the Red Dead Wiki breed tables and were checked against max-bond payouts reported by players.
