How to read this map

  • Circles are approximate named areas, not exact spawn points. Look around the whole circle.
  • Herds are random, so an area can be empty when you arrive. Come back later or try a nearby circle.
  • Where circles overlap, the colour gets darker.
  • High confidence means two or more sources agree. Medium means one source. Low means no spot of its own is known for that coat, so the circles show where its breed roams.
